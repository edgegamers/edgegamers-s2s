# Code of conduct

We welcome respectful participation in edgegamers' plugin development. Be constructive, credit others' work, respect personal boundaries, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Help newcomers understand the project without belittling them.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, unwanted sexual attention, impersonation, and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces where people represent this project.

## Reporting

To request a confidential conversation, [open an issue](https://github.com/edgegamers/edgegamers-s2s/issues/new) saying only that you need a private conduct follow-up. Do not identify the affected people or publish incident details. A maintainer can arrange a private reporting channel. If the concern involves that maintainer, request an uninvolved edgegamers organization owner.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate, direct private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers consider the circumstances and impact, seek relevant accounts, and limit disclosure of private reports. They may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or remove someone from the project for serious or repeated violations. A person involved in a report should not decide its outcome.

Good-faith questions, disagreement and constructive feedback are welcome. Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern is a violation of this policy.
