Add Decluttr, an independently configurable cleanup plugin for messages, HUD, sounds, advertising and chat, with persisted default-hidden `/legs` preferences. Cooperate through optional interfaces with TTT private chat/killfeeds, Jailbreak GunGame/spy chat and MAUL managed names while preserving owner privacy and desired names. Requires s2script runtime v0.10.1 or newer.
