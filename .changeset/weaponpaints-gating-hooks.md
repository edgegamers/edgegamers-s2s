Publish an `@edgegamers/s2s-weaponpaints` contract with `OnSelectionRequested` and `OnApplyRequested` veto hooks and a `refresh(slot)` method. A vetoed selection is not saved; a vetoed apply shows the default item and keeps the saved row. Without a listener nothing changes.
