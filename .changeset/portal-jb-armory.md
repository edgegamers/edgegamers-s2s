New Jailbreak event `jb.armory`: a prisoner reached the armory (CT spawn), once per prisoner per round and only in normal mode (no special days, LR or last guard). The armory is the map's `armory`-tagged zones when `@s2script/zones` is loaded (new optional dependency), else the CT spawn points' outline widened by 128 units. The portal counts it per round.
