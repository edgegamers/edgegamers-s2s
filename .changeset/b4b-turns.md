Shot for Shot and Mag for Mag no longer slay the shooter for "cheating" after their own shot. The turn now passes once the shooter's magazine reads empty instead of on the fire event, an out-of-turn fire only counts as cheating when that gun actually holds ammo, and the waiting player's gun is kept empty so only one player holds a bullet at a time.
