Add ArcadeScripts, an s2script port of supergosciu/ArcadeScripts-CSS. On jb_arcade_b6 it replaces the map's 29 VScript logic_scripts (Bee, Blob, Colors, Light Duel, Rogue, Tapper, Surf, Decathlon, the secret items and the rest) with TypeScript ports and answers the map's RunScriptCode inputs, so the arcade minigames work again on CS2.
