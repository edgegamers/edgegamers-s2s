Add the TTT intro and spectate hub on the GameHud. New players get the intro with Play and Spectate; the dead and spectators get a picker of the living and camera cuts between them. Play sends the player's own `jointeam`, so the round's team rules still apply. The stock team menu is turned off once a hub opens, and a server that refuses the hub gets one chat line instead.
