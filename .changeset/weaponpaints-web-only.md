With `storage = "plugin"` (the CS portal), skins are chosen on the website only. `!skins`, `!knife`, `!gloves`, `!agents`, `!music`, `!pin`, `!stattrak` and their aliases no longer open menus or change anything; they reply with the `website` link. `!wp`, `!ws`, `!kill` and `wp_refresh` stay. The in-game menus let players pick items their Dedicated Supporter tier does not cover, and those choices were saved to the portal. A reload now takes the StatTrak switch from the portal (the website's per-weapon toggle) and keeps only the session's kill counts.
