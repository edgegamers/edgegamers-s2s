## Principal findings

- Core owns lifecycle, roles, damage classification, karma, automatic RDM/slays, rules, loadouts, privacy and SQL-backed player state. Its extension forwards and natives expose these as one library. [Core API](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/core/natives.sp:1).
- Default detective threshold is six, but floor13% yields zero detectives at six or seven players and one at eight; traitors floor25% with minimum one. [Count formulas](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/ttt.sp:1496), [ratio defaults](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/core/config.sp:29).
- Core activation waits for SQL round insertion, and normal SQL connection accepts MySQL only. [Activation](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/core/sql.sp:91), [driver guard](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/ttt_sql.sp:133).
- Deaths remain concealed until a body is identified, while killer/victim role messages are enabled and round end exposes all roles/logs. [Scoreboard privacy](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/ttt.sp:723), [death disclosure](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/ttt.sp:3359), [round reveal](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/ttt.sp:1005).

[Core RDM](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/ttt.sp:2391), [report manager](/Users/ghirakawa/projects/edgegamers/s2s/ttt/sm-ttt-bara/addons/sourcemod/scripting/ttt/ttt_rdm.sp:64).
