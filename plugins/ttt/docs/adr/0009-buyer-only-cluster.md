# ADR0009 — Cluster belongs to its buyer

Accepted October2,2026 from the user's clarification: “No it is tied to only the person who bought it.”

Cluster remains an entitlement of the buyer, consumed by that buyer's next HE detonation. A dropped or picked-up grenade does not transfer the entitlement. The buyer can consume the charge with another HE. Keep the existing real child-grenade recipe and round/map/reload cleanup.

This supersedes the earlier physical-grenade transfer proposal recorded in the interview and ADR0008. The current old-port implementation already uses the buyer charge; no gameplay implementation change or native throw-correlation prerequisite is needed for this decision. Transfer research is retained only as withdrawn history.
